# 第二部分：名言引申型作文框架与审题

## 审题三步法

**第一步：理解名言，提炼核心观点**
- 圈出名言中的关键词（名词/动词/对比关系）
- 用一句话概括名言的核心含义

**第二步：确定论证角度（选2个）**
- 角度一：为什么这个道理是对的（理论/逻辑）
- 角度二：举例证明（人物/事件/亲身经历）

**第三步：联系自身，总结升华**
- 对大学生的启示
- 用一句话回扣名言

## 标准三段式框架

```
第一段：引出名言 + 解释含义（3-4句）
  ① 引出名言（There is a widely known saying that...）
  ② 解释名言含义（In other words, ... / This saying reveals that...）
  ③ 过渡句（I firmly believe that... / History abounds with examples.）

第二段：举例论证（5-6句）
  ① 论点一 + 例证
  ② 论点二 + 例证
  （每个例证用"人名+事迹+结果"的结构）

第三段：联系实际 + 总结（3-4句）
  ① 对大学生的启示
  ② 总结回扣名言
  ③ 升华句（Only by doing ... can we ...）
```

# 第三部分：写作高分句式分类

## 一、开头段句式

### 句式1：引出名言
- There is a widely known saying that "..."
- A well-known proverb has it that "..."
- ... once remarked, "..." This thought-provoking quote conveys a profound message about ...
- When hearing the saying "...", I cannot help but reflect on its deeper meaning.

### 句式2：解释名言含义
- In other words, this saying reminds us that ...
- What this proverb tries to convey is that ...
- The essence of this quote is that ...（名言的核心在于……）

### 句式3：表明立场
- I am firmly convinced that ...
- From my perspective, this saying holds true in both theory and practice.
- There is no denying that this wisdom has been validated by countless examples.

---

## 二、中间段论证句式

### 句式4：引出例证
- A compelling example that comes to mind is ...
- History abounds with examples to support this view.
- There is no better illustration of this than the case of ...
- Take ... as an example, whose story perfectly embodies this principle.

### 句式5：人物事迹描述（万能模板）
- ..., who is widely recognized as ..., once faced ... However, through ..., he/she eventually ...
- Despite ..., ... never gave up. Instead, he/she ... It was this perseverance that led to his/her eventual success.
- Born in / Growing up in ..., ... demonstrated extraordinary ... from an early age.

### 句式6：递进论证
- Moreover / Furthermore / What's more, ...
- Not only does this apply to ..., but it also holds true for ...
- Beyond ... / Apart from ..., this principle is equally relevant to ...

### 句式7：对比论证
- Conversely / On the contrary, those who ignore this principle tend to ...
- In contrast, ... serves as a cautionary tale.
- While some may argue that ..., a closer examination reveals that ...

---

## 三、结尾段句式

### 句式8：总结回扣
- In light of the above analysis, we can safely conclude that ...
- The examples cited above convincingly demonstrate that ...
- All the evidence points to one conclusion: ...

### 句式9：联系大学生实际
- As college students, we should ...
- This wisdom is particularly relevant to us, for ...
- In our pursuit of ..., we ought to bear in mind that ...

### 句式10：升华收尾
- Only by embracing this wisdom can we truly ...
- It is high time that we recognized the significance of ...
- Let us ... so that we may ...（倒装/虚拟语气收尾）

---

## 四、高分微技能句式

### 无灵主语句（让句子更地道）
- A sense of gratitude flooded over / swept over / seized me.
- A wave of excitement surged through my body.
- The thought of ... gave me a renewed sense of purpose.
- A flicker of doubt crossed his mind.
- Nervousness and fear gave way to joy.

### 强调句
- It was her perseverance and selfless devotion that strengthened my resolve.
- It is ... that/who ...（强调任何成分）
- It was on that day that he realized what truly matters.

### 倒装句
- So fascinated was I by ... that I lost track of time.
- Only by doing ... can we ...
- Not only does ... but it also ...
- Adj. as S was, S did ...（让步倒装：Weak as Jane was, she never stopped trying.）

### 虚拟语气
- But for / Without / Had it not been for ..., I would not have ...
- Were it not for ..., ... would never have ...
- It is high time that we took action to ...

### with复合结构
- He stood there, with his heart beating wildly and his face flushing.
- She walked away, with tears streaming down her cheeks.
- With the sun setting behind the mountains, a sense of peace washed over me.

### 独立主格
- She gazed at me in disbelief, face lit up / gleaming with gratitude.
- He said, his voice brimming with pride.
- David looked at me and stood up, eyes twinkling with excitement.

## 写作范文一

**主题：坚持与毅力**
> 对应真题："The more I practice, the luckier I get."（2023年6月第二套）

There is a widely accepted saying that "The more I practice, the luckier I get." **⭐ This proverb reveals a profound truth: luck is not a matter of chance, but the inevitable outcome of persistent effort and repeated practice.**（无灵主语 + 同位语从句，开篇即亮出观点）

History abounds with examples to support this view. **Take Colonel Sanders as a compelling example.** At the age of 65, with nothing but a fried chicken recipe, he knocked on 1,009 doors before finally finding a partner willing to invest in his vision. **⭐ It was his unwavering determination in the face of repeated rejection that ultimately gave rise to the global phenomenon known as KFC.**（强调句 + 无灵主语）Similarly, the Chinese scientist Tu Youyou conducted countless experiments over decades before discovering artemisinin, a breakthrough that earned her the Nobel Prize. **⭐ Had she succumbed to frustration after her early failures, the world would have been deprived of this life-saving medicine.**（虚拟语气倒装）

**⭐ As college students, we should internalize this wisdom and recognize that every seemingly insignificant practice session is a stepping stone toward our goals.** Only by embracing the grind can we transform ordinary effort into extraordinary luck.

（约185词）

**亮点句式分析**：
- `This proverb reveals a profound truth: ...` — 冒号引出同位语，解释名言
- `History abounds with examples to support this view.` — 万能过渡句
- `It was ... that ...` — 强调句突出人物品质
- `Had she succumbed to ...` — 虚拟语气倒装，假设反面
- `every seemingly insignificant practice session is a stepping stone` — 隐喻修辞

## 写作范文二

**主题：知识的浩瀚与谦逊**
> 对应真题："What we know is a drop, what we don't know is an ocean."（2024年6月第三套）

A well-known proverb has it that "What we know is a drop, what we don't know is an ocean." **⭐ This thought-provoking metaphor reminds us that human knowledge is inherently limited, and humility in the face of the unknown is a virtue worth cultivating.**（同位语 + 并列结构）

**⭐ Copernicus serves as a striking illustration of this principle.** For centuries, people took it for granted that the Earth was the center of the universe. **⭐ Yet it was Copernicus's willingness to question this long-held assumption — to acknowledge the vast ocean of what remained unknown — that led to the revolutionary heliocentric theory.**（强调句 + 破折号插入语）His story demonstrates that **⭐ only when we admit our ignorance can we open the door to genuine discovery.**（倒装句）In a similar vein, modern scientists working on artificial intelligence constantly remind us that **⭐ for all the remarkable progress we have made, we have merely scratched the surface of what machines can achieve.**（让步状语从句）

**⭐ In light of these examples, I firmly believe that intellectual humility is not a sign of weakness, but rather the foundation upon which all meaningful learning is built.** As students, we ought to remain curious and never cease exploring the boundless ocean of knowledge.

（约195词）

**亮点句式分析**：
- `This thought-provoking metaphor reminds us that ...` — 高级引出方式
- `took it for granted that ...` — 固定搭配，表示"想当然"
- `Yet it was ... willingness to ... that led to ...` — 强调句 + 抽象名词

- `for all the remarkable progress ...` — for all表让步

When hearing the saying "The best way to predict the future is to create it," I cannot help but reflect on its deeper implications. **⭐ What this quote tries to convey is that we should not passively wait for fate to unfold, but actively shape our own destiny through deliberate action.**（主语从句 + 被动转主动）

**⭐ The story of Xu Xiake, a renowned geographer of the Ming Dynasty, perfectly embodies this spirit.** Rather than confining himself to theoretical studies, he spent over thirty years traversing the vast landscape of China on foot. **⭐ His masterpiece, *The Travel Diaries of Xu Xiake*, was not the product of idle speculation, but the direct result of decades of hands-on exploration.**（并列对比结构）**⭐ Had he merely dreamed of understanding China's geography without venturing into the wild, his invaluable contributions would never have come into being.**（虚拟语气倒装）In the modern era, Steve Jobs echoed a similar philosophy when he declared that **⭐ "people who are crazy enough to think they can change the world are the ones who do."**（引用论证）

高铁的快速发展不仅方便了人们的出行，也有力地促进了沿线地区的经济增长。许多中小城市因为高铁的开通而获得了更多的投资和发展机遇。此外，高铁作为一种高效、环保的交通方式，有助于减少碳排放，推动绿色发展。

中国高铁的成功离不开持续的技术创新和大规模的基础设施投资。未来，中国计划进一步扩展高铁网络，将其延伸至更多偏远地区，以实现更加均衡的区域发展。

**参考译文**：

China's high-speed railway has achieved remarkable development in recent years. ⭐ To date, the total operating mileage of China's high-speed rail has exceeded 40,000 kilometers, ranking first in the world.（不定式作状语 + 现在分词）The high-speed rail network covers most major cities across the country, significantly reducing travel time between cities. ⭐ The rapid expansion of high-speed rail has not only facilitated people's travel but also served as a powerful catalyst for economic growth along its routes.（not only...but also + 隐喻catalyst）Many small and medium-sized cities have gained more investment and development opportunities thanks to the opening of high-speed rail lines. ⭐ The success of China's high-speed rail is attributed to continuous technological innovation and large-scale infrastructure investment.（be attributed to 高级搭配）Looking ahead, China plans to further expand its high-speed rail network, extending it to more remote regions to achieve more balanced regional development.

**亮点标注**：

- `served as a powerful catalyst for` — 隐喻，比 "promoted" 高级
- `is attributed to` — 比 "because of" 高级的归因表达

## 翻译范文三

**主题：春节**

春节是中国最重要的传统节日，标志着农历新年的开始。每逢春节，无论身在何处，中国人都会尽力回家与家人团聚，这体现了中国文化中对家庭的高度重视。春节期间，人们会进行各种庆祝活动，包括贴春联、放鞭炮、包饺子、发红包等。年夜饭是春节最重要的仪式之一，一家人围坐在一起，共享丰盛的晚餐，象征着团圆和幸福。

近年来，随着科技的发展，春节的庆祝方式也在不断变化。例如，电子红包和视频拜年已成为新的潮流。然而，春节的核心意义——家庭团聚和对美好生活的期盼——始终未变。

**参考译文**：

⭐ The Spring Festival, the most significant traditional holiday in China, marks the beginning of the lunar new year.（同位语结构）During this festival, no matter where they are, Chinese people will make every effort to return home for a family reunion, ⭐ which reflects the paramount importance attached to family in Chinese culture.（which非限制性定语从句 + paramount高级用词）During the Spring Festival, people engage in a wide range of celebratory activities, including posting spring couplets, setting off firecrackers, making dumplings, and giving red envelopes. The New Year's Eve dinner is one of the most important rituals of the Spring Festival, ⭐ during which family members gather around the table to share a sumptuous meal, symbolizing reunion and happiness.（during which定语从句 + 现在分词作状语）

⭐ In recent years, with the advancement of technology, the ways of celebrating the Spring Festival have been constantly evolving.（with复合结构 + evolve高级用词）For example, electronic red envelopes and video calls for New Year greetings have become new trends. Nevertheless, ⭐ the quintessential meaning of the Spring Festival — family reunion and the aspiration for a better life — has remained unchanged throughout the ages.（quintessential高级用词 + 破折号插入同位语）

**亮点标注**：
- `the most significant traditional holiday` — significant比important高级
- `the paramount importance attached to family` — paramount + 过去分词短语
- `engage in a wide range of celebratory activities` — engage in + wide range 固定搭配
- `a sumptuous meal` — sumptuous比delicious/rich更正式
- `have been constantly evolving` — 现在完成进行时表持续变化
- `the quintessential meaning` — quintessential比essential/核心更精准
- `has remained unchanged throughout the ages` — throughout the ages表"千百年来"
